# 🧠 Hackathon Tips

## 🎉 1. First Rule: Have Fun

Remember - hackathons are about creativity, collaboration, and learning. Winning is great, but the real reward is building something cool and meeting awesome people along the way.

## 👥 2. Team Formation

Be open to forming a team with random people - some of the best projects come from unexpected matches.
Still, it’s smart to ask potential teammates to show you some of their previous work. It helps set clear expectations and ensures everyone’s on the same page.

## 💡 3. Project & Tech Choices

Have a clear idea of what you want to build.
Before diving in, double-check that your chosen tech stack is compatible with your target environment.
For example, if you plan to deploy on an L2 like **XYZ**, make sure all tools and integrations you need actually work there.

## 😴 4. Sleep Strategy

Don’t pull an all-nighter on the first day. Get some rest early - it’ll help you think better later.
An ideal rhythm is **two short naps (around 4 hours each)** across the hackathon. You’ll stay sharp without burning out. 😄

## 📹 5. Submission Prep

Start preparing your submission **a few hours before the deadline**.
If you need to submit a video demo, give yourself enough time to record, upload, and check everything works.

## 🤪 6. Don’t Stress About Design

If you’re building something deeply technical or experimental, it’s okay if your front end isn’t perfect.
A working prototype that showcases your idea is always more impressive than a pretty UI that does nothing!
